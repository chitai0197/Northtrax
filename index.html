Detailed Services

Architectural Drafting

We produce high-quality, comprehensive working drawings that eliminate guesswork on site. Our drafting services are tailored specifically for builders who demand precision and clarity.

  • Floor plans & elevations
  • Section details & construction notes
  • Electrical & plumbing layouts
  • NCC compliant documentation

Residential Design

From initial concept sketches to 3D visualizations, our design team helps bring your clients' visions to life while keeping structural integrity and buildability at the forefront.

  • Concept design & space planning
  • 3D Modeling & Rendering
  • Material & finish selection consulting
  • Custom home & duplex designs

Construction Estimating

Protect your margins with our accurate estimating services. We break down the costs meticulously so you can bid with confidence and manage your cash flow effectively.

  • Detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ)
  • Material & labor cost analysis
  • Preliminary cost forecasting
  • Tender preparation support

DA / CC Approvals

Navigating council requirements can be a headache. We handle the red tape, ensuring all Development Application (DA) and Construction Certificate (CC) requirements are met swiftly.

  • Council submission packages
  • Statement of Environmental Effects (SEE)
  • Coordination with external consultants (Surveyors, Engineers)
  • CDC (Complying Development Certificate) applications
