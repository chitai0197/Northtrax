Who We Are

At Northtrax, we understand that as a builder, your time is your most valuable asset. Not having an in-house drafter shouldn't slow down your projects or compromise your quality.

We serve as your reliable extension, providing fast, accurate, and fully compliant residential drafting and design services. With a deep understanding of Australian building standards (NCC), we ensure your plans are construction-ready from day one.

Detailed Services

Residential drafting

We produce high-quality, comprehensive working drawings that eliminate guesswork on site. Our drafting services are tailored specifically for builders who demand precision and clarity.

  • Floor plans & elevations
  • Section details & construction notes
  • Electrical & plumbing layouts
  • NCC compliant documentation
Townhouse & Duplex Design

We design multi-dwelling homes built for narrow and high-value blocks — from single dwellings to attached terraces — balancing buildability, privacy, and street appeal.

  • Single, attached & terrace layouts
  • Optimised floor plans for tight sites
  • Privacy & solar access planning
  • Council-ready design packages
Medium Density Development

We specialise in low-rise medium density projects — townhouses, dual occupancies, and villa developments — designed to meet NSW Low Rise Housing Diversity Code requirements.

  • Dual occupancy & multi-dwelling design
  • Low Rise Housing Diversity Code compliance
  • Yield & feasibility-driven planning
  • DA & CDC pathway documentation
3D Rendering & Visualisation

We bring designs to life with photorealistic 3D visuals before a single brick is laid — helping you sell faster and secure approvals with confidence.

  • Photorealistic exterior & interior renders
  • Marketing-ready imagery for sales
  • Design walkthroughs for clients
  • Visuals to support approval submissions
Development Consulting & Estimating

We help you understand what your site can deliver and what it will cost — combining planning advice with accurate cost estimates so you can invest with clarity.

  • Site feasibility & highest-use analysis
  • DA vs CDC pathway advice
  • Detailed quantity take-offs
  • Construction cost estimating
Property Development

We support your project end-to-end — from raw land to a completed build — managing design, approvals, and delivery so you have a single trusted partner throughout.

  • Land assessment & acquisition support
  • Full design-to-approval management
  • Builder & consultant coordination
  • End-to-end project delivery
