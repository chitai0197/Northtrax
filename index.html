Who We Are

At Northtrax, we understand that as a builder, your time is your most valuable asset. Not having an in-house drafter shouldn't slow down your projects or compromise your quality.

We serve as your reliable extension, providing fast, accurate, and fully compliant residential drafting and design services. With a deep understanding of Australian building standards (NCC), we ensure your plans are construction-ready from day one.
