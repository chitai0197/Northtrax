Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from builders regarding our drafting and estimating processes.

How long does a standard residential drafting project take?

Turnaround times vary based on the complexity of the project. A standard single-story home can take 1-2 weeks for preliminary drawings, while more complex duplexes or multi-story homes may take 2-4 weeks. We always agree on a timeline upfront and stick to it, keeping your build schedule on track.

What is included in your "Revision for your life" promise?

We understand that on-site changes happen. Our promise means we offer flexible, fast turnarounds for reasonable plan amendments without hitting you with unexpected revision fees every time a minor detail changes. We act as your ongoing drafting partner, not just a one-off service.

Do you handle council submissions (DA)?

Yes. We can manage the entire Development Application (DA) or Complying Development Certificate (CDC) process. We prepare the necessary documentation, including Statement of Environmental Effects, and liaise with council and private certifiers on your behalf.

Are your drawings NCC compliant?

Absolutely. All our drafting and design work strictly adheres to the National Construction Code (NCC) and relevant Australian Standards. We ensure that our drawings are structurally sound and ready for engineering.

What software do you use for estimating?

We utilize industry-leading estimating software to produce highly accurate Bill of Quantities (BOQ). Our estimates are detailed, transparent, and designed to help you secure tenders while protecting your profit margins.
